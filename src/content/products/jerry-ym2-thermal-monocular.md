The Jerry-YM2.0 / ELF-MU is a four-in-one multifunctional infrared thermal imaging monocular fitted with a high-performance infrared detector. It features a lightweight, compact design and long battery life, and supports four operating modes: handheld, head-mounted, thermal aiming and forward clip-on.

### Key Features

- **Four-in-one device** — supports scope, clip-on, helmet and handheld modes
- **Long operating time** — powered by a single 18650 battery for up to 10 hours of continuous use
- **Lightweight body** — the whole unit weighs only 270g, suitable for long periods of handheld or head-mounted use
- **High impact resistance** — withstands impacts up to 1000G
