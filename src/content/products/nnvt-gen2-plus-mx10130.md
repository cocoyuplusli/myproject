The NNVT Gen2+ MX10130 image intensifier tube is an 18mm non-inverting tube that uses a multi-alkali photocathode, a microchannel plate (MCP) and a phosphor screen to amplify faint ambient light into clear visible images.

**Important notice:** every image intensifier tube is supplied with its individual test report.

### Photocathode & Phosphor

The tube is based on S25 or upgraded S25R multi-alkali photocathode technology. Phosphor options:

- P43 (green phosphor)
- P45 (white phosphor)

### Figure of Merit (FOM) Options

- FOM 1400+
- FOM 1600+
- FOM 1800+
- FOM 2000+ and above

Both autogated and non-autogated versions are available to meet different operational and lighting requirements.
