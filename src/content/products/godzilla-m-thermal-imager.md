The GODZILLA-M is a multi-purpose infrared thermal imaging device with a shutterless design and a super-resolution algorithm. Fitted with a 0.6-inch 1080P OLED display, it supports app connectivity, photo capture, video recording and long battery life.

### Key Features

- **High-performance infrared detection** — 60Hz frame rate, NETD < 25mK, and smooth, latency-free imaging
- **Four-in-one device** — four wearing modes: scope, clip-on, helmet and handheld, switched in one second
- **HD display** — 0.6-inch 1920×1080 OLED
- **Compact body** — compact size and a weight of only 340g
