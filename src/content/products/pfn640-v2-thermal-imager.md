The PFN640 V2 is a 640×512 thermal imager with an F/2.5 lens and a 1024×768 AMOLED display.

- Human recognition at up to 600m and vehicle recognition at up to 1070m
- NETD ≤ 40mK at 50Hz for clear, smooth thermal images
- 1–4× electronic zoom and -4 to +4 diopter adjustment
- 64GB onboard storage and Wi-Fi connectivity
- IP67 rated, operating from -40℃ to +55℃
