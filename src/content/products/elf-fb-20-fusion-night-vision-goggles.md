The ELF-FB 20 is a brand-new generation of enhanced night vision binocular goggles. Compared with the previous generation, it delivers significant improvements in image tube compatibility, low-light and infrared imaging quality, fusion recognition algorithms, and overall size and weight — giving users better night vision performance and threat recognition to support mission success and user safety.

### Dual-band Fusion Imaging for Rapid Perception

- Upgraded night vision imaging with lower distortion and reduced chromatic aberration
- Low-latency infrared imaging, with latency reduced by 33% compared with the previous generation
- A brand-new fusion image algorithm for more precise target recognition

### Miniaturized, Lightweight Design for Wearing Comfort

A redesigned optical-mechanical structure reduces the overall size and weight, minimizing fatigue during extended use.

### Enhanced Image Tube Compatibility

Supports multiple mainstream 18mm Gen2+ and Gen3 image tube models (MX10160 and MX11769), offering greater flexibility and easier replacement.

### Comprehensive Protection

Equipped with high light protection, flip-up power-off, and power-off by turning the mount to the side.
