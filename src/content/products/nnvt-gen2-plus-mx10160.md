The NNVT Gen2+ image intensifier tube consists of three core components — a multi-alkali photocathode, a microchannel plate (MCP) and a phosphor screen — that work together to amplify available ambient light into a clear visible image.

**Important notice:** every image intensifier tube is supplied with its individual test report.

### S25 / S25R Photocathode

The tube uses S25 or upgraded S25R multi-alkali photocathode technology, providing enhanced sensitivity and reliable low-light performance.

### Figure of Merit (FOM) Options

- FOM 1400+
- FOM 1600+
- FOM 1800+
- FOM 2000+
- FOM 2300+

### Manual Gain Control

Available in 3-pin configurations, manual gain lets users adjust image brightness to suit changing lighting conditions and operational requirements.

- Fixed gain (2-pin)
- Manual gain (3-pin)

### Power Supply

Optional autogating helps the tube adapt to rapidly changing light, improving image stability and protecting the tube from excessive light exposure.

- Non-autogated
- Autogated

### Phosphor Options

- P43 green phosphor
- P45 white phosphor

### Electromagnetic Shielding

Optional electromagnetic shielding improves resistance to external electromagnetic interference, ensuring stable operation in complex environments.

### Available Formats

- MX10160 2-pin
- MX10160 3-pin
- MX10160 with electromagnetic shielding
